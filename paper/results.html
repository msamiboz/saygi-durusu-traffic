The slowdown sits on free-flowing arterials

Cells are grouped by their usual speed at 09:00. Only the 50–70 km/h group is clearly slower on 10 November; congested streets and motorways look like any other day.

Part of it is a fast 10:00 hour

The main contrast compares 09:00 with 08:00 and 10:00. On 10 November the 10:00 hour was itself fast, so the size of the estimate depends on which hours serve as the reference.

Higher-CHP neighbourhoods slow more on local streets

On slow streets, drivers are mostly local, so the neighbourhood vote is a good proxy for them. There, cells in neighbourhoods with a higher CHP share in the 2023 parliamentary election slow more at 09:00, in every year. District-level votes show no such pattern.

The data contain no stationary vehicles: the lowest speed on record is 1 km/h (6 km/h in 2020). A car standing still drops out of the hourly mean, so these minutes count braking, crawling and restarting, and bound the true delay from below.
